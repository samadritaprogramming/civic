Three Branches of Government

Estimated time: 5 minutes

Why are there three branches?

The U.S. government is divided into three branches. Each branch has different responsibilities and powers.

The Legislative Branch

The legislative branch is Congress. Congress consists of the House of Representatives and the Senate.

The Executive Branch

The executive branch is led by the president. It is responsible for enforcing federal laws.

The Judicial Branch

The judicial branch interprets laws and determines how they apply to particular cases.
